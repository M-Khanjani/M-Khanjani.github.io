    About Me¶

    I am a Water Resources Engineer and Researcher working at the intersection of hydrology and machine learning. My work focuses on applying data-driven methods to environmental and hydrological challenges — from sensitivity analysis of hydrological models to predicting water quality in urban waterways.

    I hold an M.Sc. in Water Resources Engineering and Management from Sharif University of Technology and a B.Sc. in Civil Engineering from the University of Tehran. I am currently applying to Ph.D. programs to continue developing hybrid physics–machine learning frameworks for environmental systems.

    Research Interests¶

    • Modeling & Forecasting

      Hydrological and water quality process modeling and forecasting.

    • Sensitivity Analysis

      Sensitivity analysis and optimization of environmental systems models.

    • Data-Driven Methods

      Machine learning, ensemble learning, and deep learning for water resources.
